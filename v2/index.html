About Us

TMI was formed originally in 2007, and has been playing a wide variety of classic rock songs from the 60s to the 90s ever since. We are one of Tucson's best cover bands, bringing energy and nostalgia to every stage we play.

Whether it's a bar gig, a festival, or a private event, TMI delivers a high-energy show that keeps audiences on the dance floor all night long.

Meet the Band

D

Dave

Lead Guitar, Rhythm Guitar, Keyboards, Lead Vocals

Sings lead and conveys his guitar skills with a unique ability to bring his instrument to life. Along with his formidable guitar skills, he provides searing lead vocals and tasteful keyboard parts.

J

Jennifer

Rhythm Guitar, Lead Vocals

A founding member, she launched the project in 2007, which has lasted through several iterations and comes out ahead each time. She adds that unique TMI color with her rhythm guitar and vocals.

S

Sarah

Lead Guitar, Rhythm Guitar, Vocals

Our second lead guitarist. She is every bit of the sound that she generates. The harmonized leads that she and Dave play are a signature of TMI.

A

Al

Bass, Vocals

Al's ability to lock down the rhythm and stay in the pocket with our drummer gives TMI a tailored professional sound. We're forever in his debt for stepping up when we needed him.

J

Joe

Congas, Timbales, Percussion

Our conga player is a virtuoso in his own right. He delivers a steady rhythm and flair to the group's showcase. Not only that... he's cool.

R

Robert

Drums, Lead Vocals

Plays drums and has all his life. He ties everything together and provides a solid groove that keeps the audience on the dance floor.
